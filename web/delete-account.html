Delete your account and data

How to request deletion

  1. Send a WhatsApp message to the academy at +20 111 183 5595.
  2. Write "Delete account" with the player's name and player code or registered phone number.
  3. We verify the request comes from the account holder or their parent/guardian, then delete the account.

What is deleted

Player and parent details (names, phone numbers, birth date, job), the player photo, uploaded photos, videos and payment receipts, attendance records, evaluations and app login data.

Timeline

Deletion is completed within 30 days of confirming the request.

Data we may keep

Payment and subscription records may be kept only as long as required by law for accounting, then deleted.
